## The question

How can a student team gather reliable thrust and pressure data while keeping setup time, cost, and operational risk low?

## Approach

I translated the test objectives into load cases, sensor requirements, and clear interface constraints. The structure was sized for the expected axial load with a defined safety factor, then checked for stiffness so test-stand motion would not contaminate the thrust measurement.

## Outcome

The final design separated the load path, instrumentation, and blast protection into replaceable modules. A Python pipeline synchronized sensor channels, applied calibration data, and produced a standard test report minutes after each firing.
