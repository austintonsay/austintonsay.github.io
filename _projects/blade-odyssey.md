## Project work

- Developed a heating-pad trade matrix to evaluate 3 heater options against a ~9.25W / 37.185Wh system power constraint for a 1U CubeSat gathering thermal coating data in near-space conditions at ~100,000ft.
- Designed and constructed a thermostat control loop; sized and integrated two 60 x 40 mm² heating pads at 4.2V @ 3W (0.71A) each within calculated power budget.
