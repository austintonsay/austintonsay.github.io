## Objective

Improve cruise efficiency without compromising stall behavior or the team's foam-core manufacturing process.

## Analysis

I built a staged workflow: rapid airfoil screening, lifting-line planform comparisons, and higher-fidelity checks on a short list of candidates. Each model was bounded by the same mission assumptions and verified against hand calculations before its results entered the trade study.

## Result

The selected geometry reduced predicted cruise drag while preserving target stall speed and structural volume. The work concluded with drawings, manufacturing templates, and a test plan rather than a simulation alone.
