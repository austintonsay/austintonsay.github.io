## Project work

- Designed, manufactured, and compared a reference rocket and altitude-optimized rocket powered by H-class motors on a 2-person team while obtaining L1 certifications for each member
- Analyzed and compared performance data of drag, mass, and apogee gathered from OpenRocket, ANSYS, and SolidWorks simulations to real-world flight to determine which geometrical change influenced greatest apogee increase
- Assigned design freeze dates, milestone deadlines, and overall project deadlines to account for delivery and fabrication times
