## Project work

- Developed 3 structured trade studies comparing 4-6 candidate materials each to optimize structural strength, weight, cost, and manufacturability for aircraft wing and fuselage components.
- Designed tail assembly in SolidWorks intended for 3D printing to minimize part count, enhance manufacturability, and improve aerodynamic performance
- Fabricated wing/fuselage structure using SolidWorks-driven layouts, wood/foam core construction with carbon-fiber reinforcement, and controlled surface finishing with Monokote heat-shrink covering.
